Meta-finding: three unit test fixtures encoded the wrong contracts (a positional chat_async assertion, dict-shaped classes, a bare-object search_by_label stub) — unit-green, integration-dead. The fixtures matched the code's wrong assumptions, not the real dependency's behavior.
