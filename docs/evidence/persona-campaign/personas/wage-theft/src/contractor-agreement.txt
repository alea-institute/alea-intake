INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (excerpt)

This Agreement is made March 4, 2024, between VANTERA DRYWALL & FINISHING
LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company ("Company"), and ESTEBAN
MALDONADO JR. ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES. Contractor shall perform drywall taping, mudding, sanding, and
   finishing services at such project locations and during such hours as the
   Company shall designate in its weekly schedule. Contractor shall report
   to the Company shop for transportation to job sites as directed.

2. COMPENSATION. Company shall pay Contractor at the rate of $22.00 per hour
   for hours approved on the Company's weekly hours sheet. Contractor is not
   entitled to overtime, holiday pay, or any employee benefits.

3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS. Contractor is an independent contractor
   and not an employee of Company. Contractor is responsible for all taxes.
   Company will issue IRS Form 1099 for amounts paid.

4. EQUIPMENT. Company shall make available such tools, stilts, and materials
   as are required at each job site. Contractor is responsible for the care
   of Company equipment assigned to him.

5. EXCLUSIVITY. Contractor shall give scheduling priority to Company
   projects and shall not accept work that conflicts with the Company
   schedule without prior approval of the Company.

6. TERMINATION. Company may remove Contractor from the schedule at any time
   for any reason.

AGREED:
