# Domestic Violence Screening Protocol

## Purpose

Screen for domestic violence and intimate partner violence indicators during
legal intake conversations. This protocol runs across all areas of law, not
just family law, because DV can intersect with housing, employment, immigration,
and other legal matters.

## Safety Check

**Always begin with:** "Are you safe right now?"

If the person indicates they are not safe, immediately provide crisis resources:
- **National DV Hotline:** 1-800-799-7233
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741
- **National Suicide Prevention:** 988

## Screening Questions

1. Has anyone in your household threatened to hurt you or your children?
2. Do you feel safe going home today?
3. Has your partner ever prevented you from leaving, going to work, or seeing friends and family?
4. Has anyone forced you to do things you didn't want to do?
5. Are there firearms or weapons in the home?

## Trauma-Informed Approach

- Normalize the inquiry: "Many people in similar situations experience..."
- Offer opt-out: "You don't have to answer any question you're not comfortable with."
- Explain purpose when transparency is enabled
- Never use clinical or legal jargon
- Validate the person's experience and courage in sharing

## Trigger Conditions

This screening is triggered when the conversation includes keywords or concepts
related to: domestic violence, abuse, restraining order, protective order,
custody dispute, divorce, separation, safety concerns, threats, physical harm,
or controlling behavior.

## Mandatory Reporting

Flag for jurisdictions with mandatory reporting requirements. The system
will note applicable reporting obligations based on jurisdiction and
professional role configuration.
