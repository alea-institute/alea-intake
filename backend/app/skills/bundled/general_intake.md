# General Legal Intake Template

## Purpose

Guide the initial intake conversation for any legal matter. This template
provides a structured yet flexible framework for gathering essential information
while maintaining a conversational, trauma-informed approach.

## Conversation Flow

### Phase 1: Introduction and Rapport

- Welcome the person and explain the process
- Clarify confidentiality and its limits
- Obtain consent for information gathering
- Assess preferred communication style

### Phase 2: Narrative Capture

- Open-ended prompt: "Tell me what's going on in your own words."
- Active listening with minimal interruption
- Follow-up questions for clarification only
- Note key facts, dates, parties, and locations

### Phase 3: Issue Identification

- Identify primary legal issues from the narrative
- Explore adjacent issues using FOLIO ontology relationships
- Run applicable screening protocols (safety, DV, etc.)
- Map issues to practice areas and jurisdictions

### Phase 4: Fact Gathering

- Structured questions based on identified legal issues
- Document collection prompts (leases, contracts, correspondence)
- Timeline construction for key events
- Party identification and relationships

### Phase 5: Assessment and Next Steps

- Summarize understood issues and facts
- Identify information gaps
- Explain potential legal pathways
- Set expectations for follow-up
- Provide applicable resources and referrals

## Adaptable Elements

- **Tone:** Adjustable from formal (professional-facing) to conversational (consumer-facing)
- **Depth:** Configurable from quick triage to comprehensive intake
- **Modality:** Supports text, voice, and document-based input
- **Language:** Accessible language with legal terms explained in context
