# Evidence packs

Index of UAT/campaign evidence packs for this repo. Each pack is a
self-contained, offline `pack.html` review surface (plus `manifest.json` /
`feedback.json` sidecars for agent consumption) generated by
[`tools/evidence-pack`](../tools/evidence-pack/README.md). Findings are
addressed by stable ID: `EP-<REPO>-<CAMPAIGN>-<NNN>`.

| EP-ID prefix | Campaign | Title | Rubric version | Result | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EP-INTAKE-PERSONAS` | PERSONAS | alea-intake — Persona UAT Campaign (live validation) | intake-quality-v1.1 (LOCKED) | fail (gates: 2/6, 3/6, 3/6 open — BUG-12 deadline engine + issue-completeness) | [docs/evidence/persona-campaign/pack.html](docs/evidence/persona-campaign/pack.html) |

## Conventions

- Packs live at `docs/evidence/<campaign>/pack.html`.
- A rubric version cited above must be **locked** first; amending a locked
  rubric triggers re-judging of affected findings and a new pack revision.
- `manifest.json` alongside each pack maps every EP-ID to its full content
  for programmatic resolution by agents.
