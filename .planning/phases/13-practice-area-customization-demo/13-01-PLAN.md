# 13-01 — Practice-area config schema + loader + PI seed

## Goal

Introduce a typed practice-area configuration system that (a) loads YAML files from disk at startup into an in-memory registry, (b) validates each file against a Pydantic schema, and (c) ships with a single seed practice: `personal_injury`. No HTTP surface yet — that lands in 13-02.

## Why this is the first plan

Everything else (session binding, frontend selector, Railway deploy) depends on the registry existing and a real PI config being present. Keeping this plan loader-only also means it's trivially unit-testable in isolation.

## Files to create

- `backend/app/services/intake/practice_areas/__init__.py`
  - exports `PracticeArea`, `PracticeAreaRegistry`, `load_practice_areas`
- `backend/app/services/intake/practice_areas/schema.py`
  - `PracticeArea` Pydantic model with fields:
    - `id: str` — slug, must match `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,40}$`
    - `display_name: str`
    - `welcome_message_consumer: str`
    - `welcome_message_professional: str`
    - `system_prompt: str` — used as the LLM system prompt for sessions bound to this practice
    - `key_topics: list[str]` — checklist of subjects the conversation should cover
    - `disclaimer: str | None = None` — optional sensitive-practice disclaimer (used in 13-03 if set)
- `backend/app/services/intake/practice_areas/registry.py`
  - `PracticeAreaRegistry` class — wraps a `dict[str, PracticeArea]`
  - `get(id) -> PracticeArea | None`
  - `list_all() -> list[PracticeArea]` (sorted by `display_name`)
  - `register(area: PracticeArea)` — used by loader and tests
- `backend/app/services/intake/practice_areas/loader.py`
  - `load_practice_areas(directory: Path) -> PracticeAreaRegistry`
  - Globs `*.yaml`, parses each, validates with Pydantic, registers
  - Raises `PracticeAreaConfigError` (custom exception) with file path + message on any failure — fail-fast at startup
- `backend/app/services/intake/practice_areas/configs/personal_injury.yaml`
  - Real, demo-quality content for personal injury intake (see "PI seed content" below)
- `backend/tests/test_practice_areas_loader.py`
  - Happy path: loads PI config and registry exposes it
  - Missing required field → raises `PracticeAreaConfigError`
  - Invalid `id` format → raises
  - Two files with same `id` → raises with both paths in message
  - Empty directory → returns empty registry (no error)

## Files to modify

- `backend/app/main.py` — at startup (after settings load, before app.run), call `load_practice_areas` and stash result on `app.state.practice_areas`. Wrap in try/except that logs+exits if loading fails (per fail-fast policy).

## PI seed content (paste into `personal_injury.yaml`)

```yaml
id: personal_injury
display_name: Personal Injury
welcome_message_consumer: |
  I help people understand legal options after an accident or injury. Tell me what
  happened in your own words -- where, when, who was involved, and what injuries
  resulted. You can type, record your voice, or upload documents like medical bills
  or police reports.
welcome_message_professional: |
  Personal injury intake. Capture the incident facts, injuries, treatment, and
  insurance carriers. You can use the conversational interface or switch to
  structured form.
system_prompt: |
  You are a legal intake assistant specializing in personal injury matters in the
  United States. Help the person describe their accident or injury.

  Guide the conversation through these topics, one at a time, in plain language:
    - The incident: when, where, how it happened
    - Liability: who was involved, who you believe was at fault, and why
    - Injuries: what hurt, diagnoses, ongoing symptoms
    - Treatment: ER, urgent care, follow-up providers, prescriptions, missed work
    - Damages: medical bills, lost wages, property damage, out-of-pocket costs
    - Insurance: your insurance, the other party's insurance, any claims filed
    - Statements: anything said to insurers, police, or the other party
    - Statute of limitations awareness: when did this happen

  Be empathetic. Do not give legal advice or estimate case value. If the person
  describes ongoing danger, severe injury, or imminent deadlines, acknowledge and
  note it without dramatizing.
key_topics:
  - Incident facts (when, where, how)
  - Liability and fault
  - Injuries and diagnoses
  - Medical treatment
  - Damages (medical bills, lost wages, property)
  - Insurance carriers and claims
  - Prior statements to insurers or police
  - Statute of limitations / timing
disclaimer: null
```
